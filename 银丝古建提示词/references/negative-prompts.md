## 分类禁止项

| 类别 | 关键词 |
|------|--------|
| 木纹/木材 | `wood, timber, wooden, fake wood, imitation wood,仿木` |
| 实体建材 | `tile roof, solid wall,传统墙体,实体瓦片` |
| 错误金属 | `iron art, aluminum plastic, plastic, fiberglass` |
| 错误涂层 | `silver paint, chrome mirror, CG metal, plastic metal` |
