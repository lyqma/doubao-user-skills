# 类型配方模板

可复制的结构模板。把 {占位} 换成你的内容，其他字照抄。模板面向 H3（三字段），其他模型去掉字段名直接用段落。

## 1. 豪华产品广告

```
integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Cinematic product commercial, {产品} centered on a {背景} surface.
The product rotates slowly, catching light on its {关键细节}. Macro detail of {局部特写}
with shallow depth of field. The camera performs a slow orbital arc around the product,
starting at eye level and rising to a 45-degree overhead, then pushes into the macro detail.
[Shot 2] At 00:0{切点}.000, the shot cuts to {使用场景/人群场景}，{动作}.
Smooth, no sudden moves. Studio lighting: key light from 10 o'clock with soft diffusion,
rim light from behind creating an edge glow on the {材质}. Background gradient from
{色1} to {色2}. Clean, premium palette. Product colors must be accurate.

overall_soundscape: {环境底噪} + {相机移动的轻风声} + {关键动作音}.
non_diegetic_music: {乐器} at {速度} tempo, {强弱变化}, ending on a soft sting.
```

## 2. 片头字幕 / 标题序列

```
integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Cinematic title sequence, abstract {纹理/图形} background evoking {题材}.
The title "{TITLE}" in {字体风格}, {颜色}, fades in from {方向}, letters resolving
from blur into sharp focus. {辅助元素} animate in layers with staggered timing.
The camera drifts slowly through {空间} with {纵深效果}. Occasional lens flare
as light passes the frame edge. Title text must be clean, sharp, and fully readable:
{白字黑描边 / 宽字距 / 材质克制}.

overall_soundscape: {空间底噪}，{title出现时的一记重音}.
non_diegetic_music: {风格} building in intensity, thematic sting on title reveal.
```

## 3. 电商产品展示

```
integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Product showcase, {产品} on a clean {浅色/中性} background.
The product rotates 360 degrees showing all angles on a turntable (smooth, consistent speed).
[Shot 2] At 00:0{切点}.000, the shot transitions to {使用场景}：{真实使用动作}.
[Shot 3] At 00:0{切点}.000, the camera pushes into macro detail of {关键卖点}.
End with the product + logo lockup, logo "{LOGO}" crisp and unchanged.
Accurate product colors are critical. No hands unless specified, no watermarks,
no extra products, no text distortion.

overall_soundscape: {转台马达低鸣} + {使用场景环境声}.
non_diegetic_music: Upbeat commercial instrumental, soft transition sounds.
```

## 4. 游戏 CG 过场

```
integrated_multimodal_description:
[Shot 1] Cinematic game cinematic, {环境} viewed from {视角}. {角色/生物} {核心动作}.
Particle effects: {粒子/VFX描述}. Depth through atmospheric fog layers.
The camera {运镜方式} through the environment with dynamic speed: slow for scale
reveals, fast for action beats. Screen shake on impacts. {时段/人工光源} lighting,
volumetric god rays, rim light on character silhouettes. {参考游戏} aesthetic,
high contrast for drama.

overall_soundscape: {环境音} + {技能音效} + {撞击低频}.
non_diegetic_music: Cinematic orchestral score with {元素}, deep impacts, swelling on the climax.
```

## 万能短模板（快速改）

```
{时长} cinematic {类型}：{主体} in {环境}。First {起始动作}。Then {发展动作}。
Finally {收尾动作/定格}。Camera: {一镜一运镜}。Look: {调色句}。
Sound: {对白}；{环境声}；{配乐或无配乐}。Constraints: {不变量}，{禁止项}。
```
